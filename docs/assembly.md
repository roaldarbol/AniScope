# Parts
## Frames
Frames form the top and bottom of an aniscope. They can be adapted for many use cases, and also allow for assembly of larger modules.

## Arenas
Arenas are where the experiments take place. They come in different shapes and forms.

## Adapters
Adapters hold your gear in place. Whether it is cameras, environmental sensors or lights. They are 5x5 cm by design and lets you make your own attachments.
